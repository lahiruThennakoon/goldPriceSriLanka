- (technique) started Reverse Brainstorming
- (idea) Fail mode: make it feel like an Excel sheet — rows/columns data dump, no hierarchy, everything crammed in at once
- (idea) Inverse: a proper installable PWA, not a spreadsheet — native-feeling, mobile-first
- (idea) Fail mode: let the app's calculated value silently drift from real market/jeweller prices — spot formula vs. what people actually get paid or charged locally
- (idea) Inverse: cross-check against known local sources (e.g. Raja Jewellers published gold price) to prove the app isn't deviating
- (idea) Fail mode: bury the making-charge rate — don't disclose whether it's per gram or per pavan, just show a final lump number
- (idea) Inverse: let user configure making charge as % per pavan OR fixed amount per pavan, and clearly communicate which mode + rate is active
- (idea) Fail mode: never show the user when the gold price / exchange rate was last fetched — stale data looks identical to fresh data
- (idea) Inverse: always show the conversion rate and gold price together with their fetch timestamp
- (decision) Localization/multi-language not required — target users have sufficient English literacy for a simple app
- (decision) Local-storage data loss / backup is out of scope for this brainstorming — not a concern to solve for
- (idea) Fail mode: even with correct math, a Sri Lankan gold market monopoly/cartel can set local daily rates independent of the global spot price + FX — app's 'accurate' number diverges from what the local market actually pays/charges
- (idea) Inverse: make cross-checking a serious, core feature — actively show the local reference price alongside the spot-derived price and be upfront when they diverge ('the real price is X, this calculation shows Y')
- (decision) Audience clarified: app is for gold investors, not people transacting with jewellery shops — buy/sell spread is irrelevant here
- (decision) Remove making-charge / jewellery-retail framing entirely — doesn't fit an investor tool
- (idea) Replace jewellery item categories with investment-grade gold forms: biscuits (bars), coins, and bars/moulds
- (decision) Exchange-rate source doesn't have to be the official bank rate — but whatever source is used must be genuinely accurate, needs proper analysis to pick
- (idea) Engagement: daily notification at a user-set fixed time with the current rate
- (idea) Engagement: user-configurable threshold/margin alerts when gold rate rises or falls past a set point
- (idea) Threshold alerts should support both percentage-move and absolute-LKR-amount styles, user picks
- (idea) Inverse: lead the permission request with the benefit (why notifications matter for tracking gold value) before asking, rather than an abrupt OS prompt at launch
- (insight) Synthesis: (1) trust-through-verification is the throughline — never let a number stand unverified (cross-check gold rate + exchange rate against real local references, disclose divergence, timestamp everything); (2) investor-only pivot (drop jewellery/making-charges, use biscuits/coins/bars) resolves the buy/sell-spread question too; (3) notification permission should be triggered by the user setting their first alert, not requested at launch. Chosen direction: build PRD around an investor-focused, verification-first gold value tracker
